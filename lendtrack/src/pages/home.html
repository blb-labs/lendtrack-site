FAQ

Questions partners ask

Anything else? Ask us on your demo call.

Book a demo call
Do I need finance or lending experience?

No. Your job is to spot business owners who need capital and introduce them. Our in-house funding team handles the calls, documents, underwriting and closing.

How much can I earn per referral?

You earn 30% of the total commission on every referral that funds. On average that's $1,000 to $5,000 per funded deal, depending on the deal size and product. Referrals that don't fund don't earn a commission.

What does it cost to join?

We cover licensing options on your demo call. We only bring on partners we believe can succeed, so the conversation comes first.

Why a demo call instead of a sign-up button?

Because we put a real funding team behind every partner. The call lets us show you the platform, understand your network and make sure the program is a fit before you commit.

How and when do I get paid?

Commissions are paid by direct deposit. The payment terms are set out in the referral partnership agreement you sign when you join.

Will my referrals always be credited to me?

Yes. Every business owner who applies through your page, QR code or dashboard is tracked to you, and you can follow each one in real time.

What do I actually say to a business owner?

Keep it simple and honest. Make a warm introduction and share your link. You never need to promise an approval or pick a lender. Your dashboard includes short talk tracks for every funding product.

Is LendTrack a lender?

No. LendTrack works with a network of lending partners. Lenders make all funding decisions, and a referral is never a guarantee of funding.
